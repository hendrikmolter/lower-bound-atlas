# Independent citation check for the fixed-power bounds under SH

Checked on 22 November 2026 by a Codex agent against each bound's deterministic decision problem, parameter, input length, and fixed-power quantifiers.

- **Independent Set, Vertex Cover, Clique, and 3-Coloring.** MIT Hardness Group v2, Table 1, gives the Independent Set, Vertex Cover, and 3-Coloring forward implications. Complementation transfers the Independent Set bound to Clique without increasing graph order. In the converse, Theorem 2.4 transfers failure of SH to a deterministic fixed-power algorithm for 3-SAT by variable count. Theorem 5 of Impagliazzo, Paturi and Zane gives the linear-size cardinality circuit and the equivalences between Clique, Independent Set and Vertex Cover that preserve the number of vertices. For 3-Coloring the proof of Corollary 7 in Claude’s preprint gives the fixed-color encoding with a linear number of variables. For Vertex Cover by budget, Chen, Kanj, and Jia, Theorem 2.2, supplies the kernel with at most 2k vertices.
- **Dominating Set.** The induced-grid exclusion in MIT Hardness Group v2, Table 1, applies for a sufficiently large fixed dimension for each excluded power. For the converse, Bonnet, Escoffier, Kim, and Paschos, Proposition 5, proof item 2, gives the high-degree include/exclude rule when candidates and demands are represented as the two sides of a bipartite incidence graph. Taking the degree cutoff as a small power of (n) makes the number of branches fixed-power subexponential and the total incidence length at each leaf (O(n^{1+delta})). The existing proof review checks the residual CNF and its bit length.
- **Hamiltonian Cycle, Hamiltonian Path, and Longest Path.** The induced-grid Hamiltonian Cycle exclusion uses the reduction of de Berg et al., Theorem 4.7. The parameter (|V|+|E|) is linear in the order for a fixed dimension. Splitting one vertex and adding two private leaves increases order by three and makes the corresponding spanning path equivalent. For Longest Path, use the resulting order as the requested number of path vertices. For the graph-size Hamiltonian Cycle converse, Zhou, Section 2, gives the degree and binary distance conditions. A linear-size one-hot test at each vertex and (O(log n)) gates per arc give a near-linear CNF bit length. The extra polylogarithmic factor is absorbed by a strictly smaller fixed power saving.
- **Subset Sum.** The digit construction of Etscheid, Kratsch, Mnich, and Röglin (journal Lemma 13 proof and arXiv:1507.03439v1, Section 5.3, Lemma 3) has a linear number of positive items by variables plus clauses. Each has polynomial bit length. MIT Hardness Group v2, Lemma 2.1 and Theorem 3.1, give the resulting SH implication.
