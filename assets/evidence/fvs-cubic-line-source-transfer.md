The gadget correctness relates satisfiability to existence of a Hamiltonian cycle. 2. Replace this edge by Munaro's Figure 5 gadget as in Theorem 23. The gadget has constant size and preserves connectedness, simplicity, cubicity, and bipartiteness. A Hamiltonian cycle through the chosen edge opens into a Hamiltonian path through the resulting graph. In the reverse direction the local proof forces the path endpoints into the gadget and leaves a spanning path between the old edge endpoints through the rest of the graph. Closing with the chosen edge yields the required cycle. The path proof depends on the gadget's ports and uses no embedding property of the host. Call the resulting cubic bipartite graph (H). [Munaro, Theorem 23 and Figure 5 in the author copy](https://pure.qub.ac.uk/files/186487120/dmline.pdf)
