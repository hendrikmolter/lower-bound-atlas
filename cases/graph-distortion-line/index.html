Graph Distortion into a line

Recorded parameterizationsDistortion threshold
Input
A connected finite simple unweighted undirected graph G and a positive integer distortion threshold d.
Parameter
The allowed distortion d.
Question
Does the shortest-path metric of G admit a noncontracting embedding into the line that expands no distance by more than a factor d?
