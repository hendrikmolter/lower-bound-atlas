Chromatic Number

Recorded parameterizationsTreewidth Vertex cover number
Input
A finite simple undirected graph G and a positive integer ℓ.
Parameter
The treewidth t of G.
Question
Can the vertices of G be colored with at most ℓ colors so that adjacent vertices have different colors?

Restricted problems

  • 3-Coloring (color budget ℓ = 3)
