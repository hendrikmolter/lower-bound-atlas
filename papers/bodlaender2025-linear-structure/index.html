Recorded uses

  • Chromatic Number · Theorem 9 and the construction of Lemma 4, especially Claim 12. The lemma fixes the number of colors. For source length S, taking q of order √(log S) keeps the construction polynomial. Encoding choices in base q and adding a q-clique to enforce the lists gives t log(t+2) at most a computable parameter factor times log S. · Treewidth (source construction for the unrestricted bound)
  • Independent Set · Theorem 6, logarithmic pathwidth. Treewidth is at most pathwidth, so an effective sublinear exponent in treewidth would yield an FPT algorithm for Clique. · Treewidth (unrestricted lower bound)
