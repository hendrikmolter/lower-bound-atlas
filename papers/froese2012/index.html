Vincent Froese

Preferred edition · Master’s thesis, Technische Universität Berlin (2012)

Theorem 4.1 in §4.1.1, p. 33, gives the paired binary row-address construction. The later MFCS and JCSS Hitting Set reductions use an unbounded alphabet. Chapter 4 of Froese’s 2018 doctoral thesis treats binary Hamming-distance restrictions using a different construction.
