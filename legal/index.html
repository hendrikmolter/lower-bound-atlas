Privacy

Visiting and searching. GitHub Pages hosts the Atlas and logs IP addresses for security. Searches run in your browser. Opening a shared search URL sends its terms and filters to GitHub. GitHub explains retention and international transfers in its privacy statement.

Contact and contributions. Emails are kept as needed to answer requests or meet legal obligations. GitHub issues and pull requests are public. Contributions and author credits may remain in the project history. The operator relies on legitimate interests in secure hosting, answering inquiries and preserving the research record (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Your rights. You may contact the operator to request access, correction, erasure or restriction of processing, or to object on grounds relating to your situation. You may also complain to a supervisory authority, including the Brandenburg data protection authority.
