Hypotheses

k×kk × k Clique Hypothesis

Statement

Given an explicit k×kk × k array of vertices and their edges, no deterministic 2o(k⁢log2⁢k)2^(o(k log₂ k))-time algorithm decides whether a clique meets every row.

Defining source

After ETH: the k×kk×k Clique Hypothesis · Stefan Szeider · Zenodo preprint v3, 9 November 2026 · Section 2, hypothesis definition on p. 2 and deterministic convention on p. 3 · Open full text
