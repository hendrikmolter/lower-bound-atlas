Unary Bin Packing

Recorded parameterizationsNumber of bins
Input
A list of positive integer item sizes, a positive bin capacity T and a positive bin limit k. Sizes and capacity are written in unary.
Parameter
The maximum number of available bins k.
Question
Can all items be assigned to at most k bins so that the sum of sizes in each bin is at most T?
