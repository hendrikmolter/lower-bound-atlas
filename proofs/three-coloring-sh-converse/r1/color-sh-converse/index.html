Theorem 1 · The 3-Coloring bound implies the Satisfiability Hypothesis

Earlier proof revision · Current theorem revision

Exact theorem statement

If for every fixed 0<a<10<a<1 there is no deterministic 3-Coloring algorithm in 2^{𝒪(na)O(n^a)} B^{𝒪(1)O(1)}, then SH holds.
