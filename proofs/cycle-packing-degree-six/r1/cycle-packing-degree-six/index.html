Theorem 1 · Cycle Packing at bounded degree and large girth

Exact theorem statement

Assuming deterministic FPT ≠ W[1], for each fixed 0<γ<10<γ<1 there is no deterministic effective 2o(t⁢log2⁢t)⁢N𝒪(1)2^(o(t log₂ t)) N^(O(1)) Cycle Packing algorithm by treewidth or by supplied path-decomposition width, even on connected bipartite graphs of maximum degree six, girth at least n1−γn^(1−γ), and equal treewidth and pathwidth, with an optimal certificate if supplied.
