Theorem 1 · Odd Cycle Transversal on subcubic graphs of large girth

Exact theorem statement

The graph is connected and has maximum degree at most three. For each fixed 0<η<10 < η < 1 the girth is at least n1−ηn^(1−η), where n is the number of vertices.
