Theorem 1 · cs-balanced

Under deterministic FPT ≠ W[1], on inputs with L=d+1L=d+1 and used alphabet size q=2⁢d+3q=2d+3 there is no deterministic solver in 2^{h(d)h(d)} NCN^C for fixed C, where h has a computable upper bound and is effectively sublinear in d.
