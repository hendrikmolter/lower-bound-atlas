Theorem 1 · graph-distortion-line-w1

Under FPT ≠ W[1], no deterministic algorithm decides integer or real line distortion in time 2h(d)⁢B𝒪(1)2^(h(d)) B^(O(1)) on connected simple unweighted bipartite graphs when h is finite, nonnegative, and effectively o(d⁢log2⁢d)o(d log₂ d).
