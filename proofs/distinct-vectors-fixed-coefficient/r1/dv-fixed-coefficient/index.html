Theorem 1·A1 · A double-exponential lower bound for Distinct Vectors under FPT ≠ W[1]

Exact theorem statement

For every fixed 0<γ<10 < γ < 1, under FPT ≠ W[1] no deterministic algorithm decides Distinct Vectors in time 2𝒪(2γ⁢k)⁢N𝒪(1)2^(O(2^(γ k))) N^(O(1)).
