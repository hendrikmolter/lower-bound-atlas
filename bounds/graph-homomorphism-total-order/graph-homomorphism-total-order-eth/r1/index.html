Graph Homomorphism under ETH: Subexponential-time bound in total order

Statement

No deterministic algorithm solves the stated decision problem within the stated running time.

2o(p⁢log2⁢p)⁢B𝒪(1)2^(o(p log₂ p)) B^(O(1))

  • p — sum of the orders of G and H
  • B — complete explicit binary input length

Apply the source theorem with h(n)=nh(n)=n and p≤2⁢np≤2n. A fixed polynomial in the explicit graph encoding is absorbed by the positive-rate gap.
