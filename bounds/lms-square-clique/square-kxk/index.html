Defining lower bound under k×kk × k Clique Hypothesis

Statement

No deterministic algorithm decides k×kk × k Clique within the stated running time.

2o(k⁢log2⁢k)⁢N𝒪(1)2^(o(k log₂ k)) N^(O(1))

  • k — number of rows in the square grid
  • N — Input length in bits.

This exclusion is the defining statement of the k×kk × k Clique Hypothesis.
