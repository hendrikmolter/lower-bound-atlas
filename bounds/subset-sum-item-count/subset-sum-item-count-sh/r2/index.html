Subset Sum by item count under SH

Statement

Problem domain
  • Each item and the target is strictly positive. Equal item values may occur at different indices.

No deterministic algorithm decides Subset Sum within the stated running time.

2𝒪(p1−ε)⁢B𝒪(1)2^(O(p^(1−ε))) B^(O(1))

For each fixed ε strictly between zero and 1.

  • p — number of indexed input items
  • ε — a fixed positive saving in the exponent
  • B — complete input length in bits

SH ⇒ Statement

  • Polynomial kernels for weighted problems · Michael Etscheid, Stefan Kratsch, Matthias Mnich, Heiko Röglin · Journal of Computer and System Sciences 84 (2017), pp. 1–10 · §3, Lemma 13 proof, p. 8
  • ETH is Dead, Long Live SH · MIT Hardness Group, Josh Brunner, Erik D. Demaine, Fabian Frei, Timothy Gomez, Markus Hecher, Jeffery Li, Jayson Lynch · arXiv:2611.02650v2, 16 November 2026 · Lemma 2.1 and Theorem 3.1, pp. 5–6 and 8 · Open full text
Derivation from the cited sources

The digit reduction produces 𝒪(v+m)O(v+m) positive binary items for v variables and m clauses. A fixed power saving in the item count would give the same power saving in v+mv+m for 3-SAT.

Recovery of a historical ETH bound

Exponent degree recovered · ETH target: Sublinear exponent in the number of items

The SH bound excludes every fixed power below one in the exponent of the item count.
