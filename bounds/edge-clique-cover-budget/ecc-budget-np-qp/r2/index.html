Problems / Edge Clique Cover

Polynomial powers in the exponent under NP ⊈ QP

Statement

No deterministic algorithm decides Edge Clique Cover within the stated running time.

2k𝒪(1)⁢n𝒪(1)2^(k^(O(1))) n^(O(1))

  • k — allowed number of covering cliques
  • n — the number of vertices of G

Equivalence to NP ⊈ QP

NP ⊈ QP ⇒ Statement

  • Known Algorithms for Edge Clique Cover Are Probably Optimal · Marek Cygan, Marcin Pilipczuk, Michał Pilipczuk · SIAM J. Comput. 45(1) (2016), 67–83 · Theorem 1 and Corollary 3, p. 70

Statement ⇒ NP ⊈ QP

  • Data Reduction and Exact Algorithms for Clique Cover · Jens Gramm, Jiong Guo, Falk Hüffner, Rolf Niedermeier · ACM Journal of Experimental Algorithmics 13 (2009), Article 2.2 · §2, Rules 1 and 3′ and Theorem 1, pp. 2.2:4–2.2:7
Derivation from the cited sources

The kernel has at most 2k2^k vertices and therefore 2^{𝒪(k+1)O(k+1)} bits. Under NP ⊆ QP, solving this NP decision problem on the kernel takes 2^{k^{𝒪(1)O(1)}} time, in addition to polynomial preprocessing.
