Subexponential bound in k⁢log⁢kk log k under ETH

Statement

No deterministic algorithm solves Maximum Common Induced Subgraph within the stated running time.

2o(k⁢log2⁢k)⁢N𝒪(1)2^(o(k log₂ k)) N^(O(1))

  • k — sum of the minimum vertex-cover sizes of G₁ and G₂
  • N — total bit length of the input

Corollary 13 gives the parameter dependence. Theorem 12 uses a reduction of polynomial size in the source clique parameter, so adding a fixed polynomial input factor preserves the contradiction.

ETH ⇒ Statement

  • On the Complexity of Various Parameterizations of Common Induced Subgraph Isomorphism · Faisal N. Abu-Khzam, Édouard Bonnet, Florian Sikora · Theoretical Computer Science 697 (2017), pp. 69–78 · Theorem 12, pp. 74–75, and Corollary 13, p. 76
