Problems / Unary Bin Packing

Joint item-count and capacity bound under ETH

Statement

No deterministic algorithm solves Unary Bin Packing within the stated running time.

2o(n)⁢To(k)2^(o(n)) T^(o(k))

  • n — number of items
  • k — maximum number of available bins
  • T — capacity of each bin

ETH ⇒ Statement

  • Tight (S)ETH-Based Lower Bounds for Pseudopolynomial Algorithms for Bin Packing and Multi-machine Scheduling · Karl Bringmann, Anita Dürr, Karol Węgrzycki
    STOC 2026, pp. 2094–2105 · Theorem 1.1, p. 2095, and §4, pp. 2100–2102 · Open full text
    Inspected edition · arXiv:2603.12999v1 · Edition record · §8, Theorem 8.1 · The proceedings proof explicitly defers the transfer from Multiset k-way Partition with targets to Theorem 8.1 of this full version.
Derivation from the cited sources

The source measures the capacity T and the number of items n separately. Conversion of its normalized hard inputs to unary has time polynomial in n and T, which can be absorbed into the displayed little-o rates.
