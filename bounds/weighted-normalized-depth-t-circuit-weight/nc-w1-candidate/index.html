Problems / Weighted normalized circuit satisfiability

Bound in the number of circuit inputs under FPT ≠ W[1]

Statement

Problem domain
  • The circuit has depth t. Unbounded-fan-in AND and OR gates alternate, the output gate is AND, and the inputs are literals.

No deterministic algorithm decides Weighted normalized circuit satisfiability within the stated running time.

f(k)⁢no(k)⁢B𝒪(1)f(k) n^(o(k)) B^(O(1))

The circuit depth t≥2t ≥ 2 is fixed.

The function f is recursive, subject to the source’s computability and inverse-function conventions.

  • t — circuit depth
  • k — required assignment weight
  • n — number of Boolean input variables
  • B — bit length of the circuit and its weight specification, including gate identifiers and wiring

FPT ≠ W[1] ⇒ Statement

  • On the computational hardness based on linear FPT-reductions · Jianer Chen, Xiuzhen Huang, Iyad A. Kanj, Ge Xia · Journal of Combinatorial Optimization 11(2) (2006), 231–247 · Theorem 2.2 and circuit definitions, pp. 233–235, and footnote 1, p. 231
Derivation from the cited sources

The cited result assumes FPT ≠ W[t−1t−1], which follows from FPT ≠ W[1] at each of these fixed depths.
