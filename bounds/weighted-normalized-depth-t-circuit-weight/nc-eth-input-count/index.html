Problems / Weighted normalized circuit satisfiability

Bound in the number of circuit inputs under ETH

Statement

Problem domain
  • The circuit has depth t. Unbounded-fan-in AND and OR gates alternate, the output gate is AND, and the inputs are literals.

No deterministic algorithm decides Weighted normalized circuit satisfiability within the stated running time.

no(k)⁢B𝒪(1)n^(o(k)) B^(O(1))

The circuit depth t≥2t ≥ 2 is fixed.

The exponent is bounded by k/r(k)k/r(k) for an unbounded nondecreasing function r.

  • t — circuit depth
  • k — required assignment weight
  • n — number of Boolean input variables
  • B — bit length of the circuit and its weight specification, including gate identifiers and wiring

ETH ⇒ Statement

  • Tight Lower Bounds for Certain Parameterized NP-Hard Problems · Jianer Chen, Benny Chor, Mike Fellows, Xiuzhen Huang, David Juedes, Iyad A. Kanj, Ge Xia · Information and Computation 201(2) (2005), 216–231 · Theorem 3.3, p. 224, and the circuit definitions in Section 2, pp. 219–220
Derivation from the cited sources

ETH implies FPT ≠ W[1]. The W-hierarchy inclusion then gives the premise of the cited input-count theorem.
