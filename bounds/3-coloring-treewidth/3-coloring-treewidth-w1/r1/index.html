FPT ≠ W[1] ⇒ Statement

  • XNLP-Completeness for Parameterized Problems on Graphs with a Linear Structure · Hans L. Bodlaender, Carla Groenland, Hugo Jacob, Lars Jaffke, Paloma T. Lima · Algorithmica 87 (2025), 465–506 · §4.3, Lemma 4 and Claim 12
Derivation from the cited sources

The logarithmic-pathwidth hardness gives a fixed-parameter reduction from Clique whose output pathwidth is at most a computable parameter factor times the logarithm of the graph order. Treewidth is at most that pathwidth. The effective cutoff would turn the claimed solver into a fixed-parameter algorithm for Clique.
