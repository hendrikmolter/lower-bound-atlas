Problems / 3-Coloring

Subexponential bound in treewidth under ETH

Statement

No deterministic algorithm decides 3-Coloring within the stated running time.

2o(t)⁢n𝒪(1)2^(o(t)) n^(O(1))

  • t — treewidth of G
  • n — number of vertices of G

ETH ⇒ Statement

  • Lower bounds based on the Exponential Time Hypothesis · Daniel Lokshtanov, Dániel Marx, Saket Saurabh · Bulletin of the EATCS 105 (2011), 41–71 · Theorem 3.2, PDF p. 12 · Open full text
Derivation from the cited sources

A treewidth-subexponential algorithm would also be subexponential in the number of vertices, since treewidth is at most the graph order.
