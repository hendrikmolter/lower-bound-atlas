Connected Vertex Cover under ETH: Subexponential-time bound

Statement

No deterministic algorithm solves the stated decision problem within the stated running time.

2o(k)⁢n𝒪(1)2^(o(k)) n^(O(1))

  • k — solution budget
  • n — number of vertices

ETH ⇒ Statement

  • Domination When the Stars Are Out · Danny Hermelin, Matthias Mnich, Erik Jan van Leeuwen, Gerhard J. Woeginger · ACM Transactions on Algorithms 15(2) (2019), Article 25 · Proof of Theorem 10.8 · Open full text
  • Which Problems Have Strongly Exponential Complexity? · Russell Impagliazzo, Ramamohan Paturi, Francis Zane · JCSS 63(4) (2001), 512–530 · Corollary 1 · Open full text

Source note

The proof first gives the linear-budget reduction to Connected Vertex Cover and then uses it for an edge-domination result.
