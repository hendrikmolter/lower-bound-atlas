Exponential dependence on treewidth under ETH

Statement

No deterministic algorithm decides Vertex Cover within the stated running time.

2o(t)⁢B𝒪(1)2^(o(t)) B^(O(1))

  • t — treewidth of the input graph
  • B — complete binary input length

ETH ⇒ Statement

  • Which Problems Have Strongly Exponential Complexity? · Russell Impagliazzo, Ramamohan Paturi, Francis Zane · JCSS 63(4) (2001), 512–530 · Corollary 2, p. 524, and Theorem 5, p. 526 · Open full text
  • Lower bounds based on the Exponential Time Hypothesis · Daniel Lokshtanov, Dániel Marx, Saket Saurabh · Bulletin of the EATCS 105 (2011), 41–71 · Theorem 3.3, p. 52 · Open full text
Derivation from the cited sources

The Vertex Cover instances produced from sparse 3-SAT have linear graph order, and treewidth is bounded by that order. A solver with the stated dependence on treewidth would solve sparse 3-SAT in subexponential time.
