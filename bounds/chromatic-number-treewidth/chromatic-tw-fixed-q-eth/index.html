Subexponential bound in treewidth under ETH · Fixed number of colors

Statement

Input restrictions
  • The input color budget is the fixed integer q.

No deterministic algorithm decides Chromatic Number within the stated running time.

2o(t)⁢n𝒪(1)2^(o(t)) n^(O(1))

For every fixed integer q≥3q ≥ 3.

  • t — treewidth of G
  • n — number of vertices of G

ETH ⇒ Statement

  • Lower bounds based on the Exponential Time Hypothesis · Daniel Lokshtanov, Dániel Marx, Saket Saurabh · Bulletin of the EATCS 105 (2011), 41–71 · Theorem 3.2, PDF p. 12 · Open full text
Derivation from the cited sources

For fixed q, add a clique of q−3q−3 vertices adjacent to every vertex of the 3-Coloring instance. These vertices use q−3q−3 colors and increase treewidth by at most q−3q−3. The new graph has at most q colors exactly when the original graph has at most three.
