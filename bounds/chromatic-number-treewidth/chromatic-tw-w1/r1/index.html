Effective little-o bound in the treewidth under FPT ≠ W[1]

Statement

No deterministic algorithm decides Chromatic Number within the stated running time.

2oeff(t⁢log2⁢t)⁢N𝒪(1)2^(o_eff(t log₂ t)) N^(O(1)) Effective little-o

  • t — treewidth of G
  • N — complete binary input length

FPT ≠ W[1] ⇒ Statement

  • XNLP-Completeness for Parameterized Problems on Graphs with a Linear Structure · Hans L. Bodlaender, Carla Groenland, Hugo Jacob, Lars Jaffke, Paloma T. Lima · Algorithmica 87 (2025), 465–506 · Theorem 9, Lemma 4 and Claim 12.
Derivation from the cited sources

Lemma 4 fixes the number of colors q. In its construction for a Chained Positive CNF-SAT instance of length S and parameter k, choose q of order √(log S). Encoding the choices in base q uses at most 1+logq⁢S1+log_q S digits per group. The connector branches can be processed separately, so Claim 12 bounds the width by 2⁢k(1+logq⁢S)+32k(1+log_q S)+3. Adding a clique of q colors enforces the lists at an additional width cost of at most q. The graph still has polynomial size, and t⁢log2(t+2)t log₂(t+2) is at most a computable function of k times 1+log2⁢S1+log₂ S. Composing with the Clique reduction supplied by Theorem 9, the effective cutoff would turn a solver within the displayed time into an FPT algorithm for Clique.
