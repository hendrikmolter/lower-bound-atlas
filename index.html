What do these recovery statuses mean?
Recovered
The alternative bound implies the full historical ETH bound.
Effectively recovered
The historical running-time scale is excluded for algorithms with effective little-o convergence.
Recovered up to a logarithmic factor in the exponent
The excluded exponent scale loses one logarithmic factor. For a tower, the loss is in its innermost exponent and the height stays the same.
Exponent degree recovered
If the historical bound excludes 2o(ka)2^(o(k^a)) time, the recovered bound excludes 2𝒪(ka−ε)2^(O(k^(a−ε))) time for every fixed 0<ε<a0 < ε < a. For a tower, this applies to its innermost exponent at the same height.
Exact recovery ruled out by equivalence
The historical bound is equivalent to a refuted ETH formulation. Recovering it exactly would imply that the selected hypothesis is false.
Unresolved
A lower bound is recorded under the alternative hypothesis, but the comparison with this historical target is unresolved. A hardness-only notice does not qualify.

A partial recovery can also require effective little-o. An equivalence barrier can coexist with partial recovery. Problems without a historical ETH target do not match this filter.
